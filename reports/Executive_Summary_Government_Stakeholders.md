### 3. Granular Analysis of the 5 Core Segmentation Dimensions

#### 3.1 Geographic Segmentation (France, Spain, Germany)
- **France (5,014 accounts, 50.1%):** 16.15% churn rate. 48.2% maintain zero balance (€0). Total lost capital: €49.0M.
- **Germany (2,509 accounts, 25.1%):** **32.44% churn rate.** 100.0% hold active balances (€119,730 mean). Total lost capital: **€101.4M**.
- **Spain (2,477 accounts, 24.8%):** 16.67% churn rate. 48.4% zero-balance accounts. Total lost capital: €35.2M.

#### 3.2 Age Segmentation (<30, 30–45, 46–60, 60+)
- **<30 yrs (Youth, 1,641 accounts):** 7.50% churn rate (Optimal stability).
- **30–45 yrs (Core Working, 6,108 accounts):** 15.39% churn rate.
- **46–60 yrs (Peak Wealth, 1,787 accounts):** **49.36% churn rate (Critical Crisis Cohort).**
- **60+ yrs (Seniors, 464 accounts):** 19.83% churn rate.

#### 3.3 Credit Score Bands (Low, Medium, High)
- **Low (<580, 1,408 accounts):** 22.02% churn rate.
- **Medium (580–669, 3,747 accounts):** 20.44% churn rate.
- **High (670+, 4,845 accounts):** 19.83% churn rate.

#### 3.4 Customer Tenure Groups (New, Mid-Term, Long-Term)
- **New (0–2 yrs, 2,490 accounts):** 20.84% churn rate (Onboarding friction).
- **Mid-term (3–6 yrs, 4,008 accounts):** 20.31% churn rate.
- **Long-term (7–10 yrs, 3,502 accounts):** 20.07% churn rate.

#### 3.5 Balance Segments (Zero-Balance, Low-Balance, High-Balance)
- **Zero-Balance (€0, 3,617 accounts):** 13.82% churn rate (Low capital impact).
- **Low-to-Medium Balance (<€100k, 1,584 accounts):** 20.77% churn rate.
- **High-Balance (>€100k, 4,799 accounts):** **25.23% churn rate (€159.5M balance loss).**

---

### 4. Structural Attrition Anomalies
1. **The Multi-Product Holding Cliff:** 2 products yields lowest churn (7.58%), but holding 3 products surges to 82.71% and 4 products hits **100.0% complete churn (60/60)**.
2. **The German Balance Paradox:** Germany has highest wealth accumulation (€119.7k avg) and highest churn (32.44%), generating €101.4M direct capital loss.

---

### 5. Regulatory & Supervisory Recommendations
1. **Adopt Evidence-Aware Retention Allocation (EARA):** Prioritize capital preservation over naive probabilities, protecting 18.4% more balance under equal budget.
2. **Mandate Multi-Product Sales Quota Audits:** Restrict aggressive bundling until onboarding service quality is resolved.
3. **Dedicated Private Wealth Retention in Germany:** Deploy proactive relationship management for depositors aged 45–60 with €100k+ balances.
4. **Algorithmic Fairness Compliance:** Ensure AI models adhere to the EU AI Act and Four-Fifths Rule fairness across sovereign territories.
